About Amos Garden

Your trusted plant shop in Parañaque City

Our Story

Amos Garden was founded in 2015 with a simple mission: to bring the joy of plants to every home.

Started by plant enthusiasts and horticulturists, we've grown from a small nursery to a trusted name in Parañaque City's plant community. Our journey began with a small storefront where we curated the finest plants and gardening supplies for our community.

Today, we proudly serve thousands of customers who appreciate the beauty and benefits of having plants in their homes and workplaces. We're committed to sustainability, quality, and providing our customers with expert advice.

Our Mission & Vision

What drives us forward

Our Mission

To provide customers with healthy, beautiful plants and gardening supplies that enhance the quality of life in their homes, offices, and gardens. We're committed to education, sustainability, and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become the premier destination for plants and gardening in the Philippines, recognized for quality, innovation, and customer care. We imagine a society where nature and plants are integral to every living space.
